Safety review · case 001

🧬 Case 001: FGF-Restore

A trial that met its primary endpoint, and a participant who did not reach puberty.

Your role. You are a reviewer on a regulatory safety panel. A sponsor has reported a serious adverse event in an ongoing gene editing trial and submitted the packet below. Your job is not to design anything. It is to work out, from the sponsor's own data and public databases, what happened at the molecular level, and to make a recommendation.

The submission

FGF-Restore is an open-label phase 1 trial in children with achondroplasia. Achondroplasia is caused by an overactive FGFR3 receptor, which restrains growth plate cartilage. The sponsor delivers SpCas9 and a single guide RNA by AAV to disrupt FGFR3 in growth plate chondrocytes and relieve that restraint.

Fourteen participants were dosed between 12 and 24 months of age. The primary endpoint was met: mean annualised growth velocity increased and was sustained through year 3. No infusion reactions, no hepatotoxicity, no deaths.

SPONSOR Meridian Skeletal Therapeutics
PRODUCT MST-114, AAV9-SpCas9 + sgRNA
INDICATION Achondroplasia, ages 1 to 2
TARGET FGFR3
PROTOSPACER 5'-TGGAGATGATGAAGATGATC-3'
PAM GGG
NUCLEASE SpCas9, wild type
SPECIFICITY "No off-targets of concern were identified."
METHOD in silico prediction, restricted to the FGFR3 locus
The sponsor's specificity assessment is quoted verbatim.

Serious adverse event report

Participant 007. Male, dosed at 19 months. Growth response among the best in the cohort. Now aged 13.

He has not entered puberty. He cannot identify any of six standard test odours. Serum gonadotropins are low, testosterone is prepubertal, and a GnRH stimulation test is blunted. Pituitary MRI is structurally normal. Karyotype is 46,XY.

Two further participants, both now over 11, have no secondary sexual characteristics. The data monitoring committee has paused enrolment.

Tools

CRISPOR or UCSC BLAT against hg38, plus Ensembl, NCBI Gene and GTEx.

Before you start

Not graded, just for stats. Fill in each box as you go, then submit once at the end.

1Where does this guide actually cut?

Run the protospacer against the human genome (hg38). Report every site that is a perfect match with a valid SpCas9 PAM.

2What did the sponsor miss?

3Does that explain the adverse event?

Cas9 produces indels, so the consequence at the unintended site is loss of function. Look up what losing that gene does.

4Root cause

The two sites are an exact 20 out of 20 match. That is not bad luck. Work out why these two genes share this sequence, and why a guide designed against one was always going to cut the other.

5Your determination

What should happen to this trial?
