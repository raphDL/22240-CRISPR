Safety review · case 002

🧬 Case 002: Alveon-CF

The edit was confirmed. The protein never appeared.

Your role. You are a reviewer on a regulatory safety panel. A sponsor has reported a serious adverse event in an ongoing gene editing trial and submitted the packet below. Your job is not to design anything. It is to work out, from the sponsor's own data and public databases, what happened at the molecular level, and to make a recommendation.

The submission

Alveon-CF is a phase 1 trial of a cytosine base editor delivered to airway epithelium by lipid nanoparticle. The participant carries a known pathogenic CFTR variant. A base editor was chosen rather than a nuclease because the correction needed is a single base and no double-strand break is required.

SPONSOR Alveon Bio
PRODUCT ALV-02, LNP, BE4max cytosine base editor + sgRNA
EDITOR C>T, stated activity window = protospacer positions 4 to 8
VARIANT NC_000007.14:g.117480096T>C
CLINVAR Pathogenic (VCV000053622)
INTENT revert the patient's C back to the reference T
PROTOSPACER 5'-GACCACGCAGAGGTCGCCTC-3'
PAM TGG
STRAND plus
START chr7:117,480,091
QC RESULT amplicon sequencing, bronchial brushing, day 28
  g.117480096 C>T in 71% of alleles. Intended edit confirmed.
ASSAY SCOPE base call reported at g.117480096
The sponsor's QC method and its scope are quoted verbatim.

Serious adverse event report

Participant 004. The intended edit was achieved in 71% of alleles, well above the sponsor's 30% threshold for a predicted clinical benefit.

Sweat chloride is unchanged at 102 mmol/L. FEV1 is unchanged. Western blot of brushed airway epithelium detects no CFTR protein of any size, including no truncated species on a gel run to resolve low molecular weight bands.

The sponsor proposes that delivery was insufficient and requests permission to escalate the dose. The data monitoring committee has asked for an independent review first.

Tools

Ensembl or the UCSC Genome Browser to pull the locus, Benchling to lay out the protospacer, and ClinVar.

How do I get the sequence?

In the UCSC Genome Browser on hg38, enter chr7:117,480,060-117,480,130 and use View → DNA. Or in Ensembl, use the same region and Export sequence. Paste the result into Benchling as a plain DNA sequence, then find the protospacer in it.

Careful: the reference genome carries the healthy base. The patient does not. Make sure the sequence you reason about is the patient's.

Before you start

Not graded, just for stats. Fill in each box as you go, then submit once at the end.

1Verify the intended edit

Pull the locus, write out the patient's sequence, and locate the protospacer. Then work out what the sponsor's intended correction actually restores.

2Audit the editing window

The editor converts cytosines, and it cannot be aimed at one of them. Mark protospacer positions 4 to 8 on the patient's sequence and list every C in that window.

3Identify the consequential one

Look up each coordinate from step 2. One of them is in ClinVar in its own right.

4Reconstruct what the patient actually received

Write out the edited allele with every window cytosine converted, then translate from the start codon. The reference protein is 1,480 amino acids.

5Your determination

What should happen to this trial?
